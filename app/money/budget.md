name: Income, Fixed, Tuition, Emergency, Investment, Electricity, Phone,

## Allocation order (each payday)
1. Fixed monthly expenses
2. Sinking funds (tuition, yearly costs)
3. Emergency fund top-up (until target reached)
4. Investment
5. Living costs (variable)
6. Leftover → wishlist fund
